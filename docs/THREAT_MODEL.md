# Data and authority boundaries

## Present tooling

Maintained source, selected executable files and the locked tooling environment are inputs to repository controls. Received descriptors and generated inventories cannot select policy or start acquisition. Checks inspect bounded local scopes and report fixed diagnostics. Byte identity is separate from interpretation and physical qualification.

An inventory is not a concurrent filesystem snapshot or power-failure durability guarantee. Native children use finite argument vectors and direct-child deadlines; completed-output limits are not live volume ceilings. Foundation qualification is in progress.

## Future observation inputs

Plans, raw observations, conditions, calibration records and reports are supplied data. They do not grant device access, program execution, signing, equipment control, provider access, spending or publication authority. Actual source, observer, raw decoder and instrument admission precede use.

Preserve complete event identity and classification, clock/reset mappings, observed windows, gaps and jointly acknowledged checkpoints. No silent double-count, gap-as-exposure, fabricated event-free interval, erased failed attempt or invented finite upper bound is admissible.

Unknown law, detector coverage, physical clock, numerical method or current trust cannot become positive acceptance. A signature does not establish numerical validity or physical reliability. Shared evidence, equipment and operational recovery remain separate unimplemented domain boundaries; no isolation, scientific or durability guarantee is claimed here.
