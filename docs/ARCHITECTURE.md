# Repository architecture

## Current executable tooling

Metadata, source-provenance, workflow, dependency-alignment, documentation, inventory and preflight controls inspect their declared local scopes. Native-tool bootstrap checks selected executable and licence files. Received metadata cannot choose policy or grant reliability acceptance. Foundation qualification is in progress.

## Intended observation boundaries

| Component | Responsibility | Required evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admission | Complete prior workload, device, observer, clock, condition and stopping plan | Exact source, compiler, bitstream, weights, seeds, checker, mitigation, population and limits |
| Runner | Bounded soak start and new identified segment resume | Actual admitted adapter, request identity, durable receipts, reset epoch and predecessor |
| Journal | Joint event, observed-window, gap, condition and checkpoint commits | Full immutable raw bytes, event identity and acknowledged ranges |
| Accounting | Reconstruct disjoint device exposure and observed event classes | Common clock mapping, actual devices, complete coverage and classification protocol |
| Inference | Admitted law, numerical enclosure, drift and budget | Independent methods, stopping assumptions, physical uncertainty and joint error allocation |
| Report | Full native record through installed shared provider | Same source and raw references, independent owner and current verification |
| Consumers | Independently verify and render existing findings | Installed separate consumers, exact lab limits and actual Studio journey |

The planned report has fourteen required members: schema_id, claims, source_ref, environment_ref, design, workload, tmr, segments, counts, strata, condition_control, drift, budget and verdict. This is a declaration, not registered or qualified support.

Five prospective library operations are start_soak, resume_identified_segment, analyse, report and verify. A future run command composes start or resume with bounded inspection, stop request and segment closure. Direct start-soak and resume-identified-segment command spellings remain prospective. None is installed or advertised as available. Starting acquisition does not complete a soak.

All observation windows are half-open. Committed observed windows and explicit gaps partition each extent; downtime and uncommitted tails are excluded. Map every clock and reset epoch to a prior common epoch before checking same-device overlap across segments. Different devices may overlap. Exposure counts each disjoint committed physical device window once using its qualified clock conversion. Zero exposure cannot estimate a rate.

Commit event bytes, coverage, conditions and checkpoint together; neither numerator nor denominator can be acknowledged alone. Only identical complete acknowledged event replays deduplicate. Conflicting identity/content refuses; distinct events sharing a timestamp remain distinct. Resume creates a new identified segment with predecessor and explicit gap, preserving acknowledged history.

Observed classes are corrected-by-TMR, detected-uncorrected, suspected-silent and voter-only. Voter flag is separate. Corrected requires TMR and flag; voter-only requires flag; non-TMR cannot claim corrections or interventions. The selected observed-error convention includes suspected candidates explicitly. It does not identify a population silent-error rate without an admitted detection model. A held combinational disagreement is not automatically a new physical event each sample.

Prior strata bind design, workload, TMR, devices, conditions, detector, counting and stopping. Homogeneous independent Poisson inference requires an admitted event law and complete fixed exposure. Clustering, nonstationarity, informative censoring and optional stopping invalidate that simple interpretation. Preserve descriptive counts and exposure; an alternative needs its own qualified method. Zero observations imply an upper-bound interpretation only under the selected admitted assumptions.

Central and explicitly one-sided zero-count intervals are different methods. Tail selection belongs to the prior exact method reference. A future numerical backend must retain exact count and probability inputs, validated brackets, rounding and error traces. Finite reference agreement does not prove a whole-domain enclosure. Physical clock uncertainty propagates into exposure and rates; zero lower exposure makes the rate upper unbounded and unavailable.

Signed drift is later minus earlier over full paired windows. Relative change is unavailable when its qualified baseline contains zero. State drift requires complete paired states, units and normalisation; missing states are not zero drift. Temperature is recorded with calibration and scope, not controlled or evidence of causality.

No budget gives a descriptive verdict. A prior budget binds population, strata, threshold, inclusive or exclusive comparison and joint confidence allocation. Within-budget needs every conservative upper to satisfy the threshold; exceeded needs a conservative lower to contradict it. Equality follows the declared comparator. Missing, ambiguous or unbounded quantities remain inconclusive. Marginal confidence does not establish whole-report confidence.

Raw bytes precede inventories, analysis, native report, shared manifest, signatures and later receipts. Native verdict, operation completion and current eligibility are separate. No signature, matching hash, authored fixture or simulated fault establishes physical reliability. Recorded conditions, lab baselines and field observations retain their separate populations.
